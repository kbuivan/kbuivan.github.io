---
title: "Silence is golden"
ref: de-silence
line: digital-exhaustion
---

I grew up with the idea that “speech is silver, silence is golden”, and that to live happily, live quietly.<br>
Today, those reference points have been shattered. On social and professional networks, visibility has become close to a prerequisite for social and economic survival. Silence, once a refuge and a mark of wisdom, is now read as absence, or even as a kind of intellectual poverty.<br>
I wonder whether this constant demand to speak, to show, to perform is exhausting us collectively. What if real maturity, for our generation, lay precisely in reinventing that balance: knowing when to stay silent strategically, and when to speak up with conviction?<br>
Not entirely hidden. Not constantly overexposed. Just… deliberate.
