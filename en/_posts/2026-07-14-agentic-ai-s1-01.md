What we are experiencing today is not just a technological evolution — it is a historic shift.

Agentic AI is arriving faster than any previous revolution. Autonomous agents capable of executing, learning, collaborating, and deciding. For the first time, it is becoming realistic to imagine more AI agents than human workers in the near future.

I have been observing this transformation for more than two years and have been actively preparing my teams for over a year. One thing is certain: the world of work will never be the same.

What is already changing today: Repetitive, administrative, and low-value-added tasks are being absorbed by AI. This is no longer a prediction — it is reality.

What every professional must do now:

- Understand the different types of AI and learn how to use them.
- Integrate AI into daily work to gain speed, reliability, and productivity.
- Cultivate uniquely human strengths: creativity, empathy, leadership, and critical thinking.

The role of leaders:

Act now! Leaders no longer have the luxury of waiting. They must prepare their teams, preserve employability where possible, build a culture where AI is a lever rather than a threat, and anticipate change instead of enduring it.

A leader who fails to guide their teams through this transition risks leaving them behind.

The environmental challenge cannot be ignored:

- Total data-center electricity: 448 TWh (2025) → ~980 TWh (2030), +119% — reaching ≈3% of global electricity (Gartner, Nov 2025).
- AI-optimized servers alone: 93 → 432 TWh over the same period (~5x), representing ~44% of total data-center power by 2030 — and 64% of the incremental demand (Gartner).
- A projected 9.3 trillion liters of water/year and 14,500 km² land footprint by 2030 (UN University – UNU-INWEH, 2026).

If we do not change our technological trajectory, we risk direct competition between AI's needs and humanity's essential resources.

Fortunately, solutions are emerging:

- Data centers powered by renewable energy or advanced modular reactors,
- Discussions around universal basic income,
- And a growing awareness that great opportunities come with great responsibilities.

A new industrial revolution — but in accelerated mode.

The key differences?

- Speed.
- Scale.
- Global impact.

But humanity has always demonstrated resilience and creativity. It will adapt once again, evolve, and create new jobs and new industries.

The time to act is now. Leaders, prepare your teams. Accelerate responsible AI adoption. Protect employability. Anticipate environmental challenges.

Be the architects of the future — not mere spectators.
