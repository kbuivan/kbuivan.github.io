Welcome.

I created this space to explore subjects that interest me, and to invite you to look at them differently.

The questions that set off my thinking on agentic AI were very down to earth.

The first: understanding and anticipating the impact of this new technology on the world of work, and more specifically in my own field, life sciences regulation. How can people remain employable? Does my network grasp how much work could change in the years ahead?

The second was more personal. As the mother of a child starting higher education, I wondered which kinds of learning would still hold their value tomorrow.

This is how AI became my first field of reflection. I soon noticed that it opened up many others, just as intriguing.

AI, organisations, languages, digital exhaustion and human value may seem to belong to separate worlds. Yet they often bring me back to the same questions: how we think, how we learn, how we decide, and what remains distinctly human as our tools evolve.

The texts gathered here are content to leave questions open. They grow out of a curiosity for implicit assumptions, rarely questioned certainties and the less visible pathways that other approaches sometimes overlook.

I like to start from a simple observation, a word, a behaviour or a widely held idea, and then follow the questions it raises. Some of these paths take me well beyond where I started, and it is this journey that I would like to share with you.

What these texts have in common is not the subject itself, but the approach: taking the time to examine what seems obvious.

I am Kimty Bui-Van, a regulatory affairs executive and a curious observer of the links between technology, organisations, cultures and human experience.

Thank you for visiting.
